Chapter One

From classmates
to everything.

We didn't meet in some grand, cinematic way. We were classmates in school — two people who saw each other every day and had no idea what was quietly building underneath all that ordinary time.

Years later, it was Instagram that brought us back into each other's world. What started as small chats turned into conversations neither of us wanted to end. On 13th August, We finally said what we have been holding back. And on 17th August, you became mine forever— officially, completely, for keeps.

Since then it's been simple, really: we fight, we forgive, we love — in that order, again and again, because neither of us knows how to let go of the other. That's not a flaw in our story. That's the whole story.

Two years later, you're still my Manchali Chulbuli Pingu Baby. And I'd choose every single one of these days again.
